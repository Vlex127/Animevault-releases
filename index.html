AnimeVault

What's inside

Built for the
serious viewer

Every feature designed around the experience of watching anime, not around looking like a dashboard.

🎬
Cinematic Player

Custom HLS player with quality selection, subtitle/dub switching, auto-skip intro/outro via AniSkip, PiP, and keyboard shortcuts.

↓
Native Downloads

Download episodes via yt-dlp with real-time progress, pause/resume support, and automatic retry on failure.

✦
AniList Discovery

Browse trending, seasonal, and genre-filtered anime powered by AniList's GraphQL API with rich metadata and artwork.

◎
Watch History

Per-episode progress saved locally. Resume from exactly where you left off across sessions.

☰
Your Library

Organize anime into My List, Favorites, Watching, Completed, and custom Collections.

▶️
AnimePahe Stream

Direct HLS streaming from AnimePahe with 360p, 720p, and 1080p quality options. Sub and dub where available.
