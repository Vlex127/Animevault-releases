What's inside

Built for the
serious viewer

Every feature is designed around the experience of watching anime — not around looking like a dashboard.

Cinematic Player

Custom HLS player with quality selection, subtitle and dub switching, picture-in-picture, keyboard shortcuts, and a theater-grade dark UI.

Auto-Skip Intro & Ending

AniSkip-powered skip buttons appear the moment an OP or ED starts — for streamed and downloaded episodes alike.

Native Downloads

Queue episodes at any resolution with yt-dlp. Pause, resume, retry, and watch offline — with clean per-show folder naming.

AniList Discovery

Trending, seasonal, and genre-filtered browsing powered by AniList — rich metadata, recommendations, and relations on every page.

Library & History

My List with watching/completed statuses, favorites, custom collections, and per-episode resume so you never lose your place.

Local Library

Point it at any folder of anime files on an external drive — it parses, enriches with AniList metadata, and plays them in-app.

Trust & safety

Hardened in v0.5.0

The update path and the streaming proxy were production-hardened — every byte that reaches your machine is verified.

SHA-256 verified updates

Every release asset carries a cryptographic digest. The installer and the in-app updater both verify the download before a single file touches your install.

Sandboxed renderer

The UI runs in a sandboxed Electron process behind context isolation — a compromised page can't reach your filesystem.

SSRF-guarded stream proxy

The local HLS proxy validates every URL and redirect hop against an internal-address blocklist, so a malicious CDN can't make the app probe your network.

Validated IPC boundary

All renderer-to-main messages pass zod schema validation at the boundary, and update downloads are pinned to GitHub's own hosts.
